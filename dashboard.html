Does a health claim mean healthier food?

The honest headline

A health claim isn't a guarantee — but it isn't a red flag either. Compared fairly against the full dataset, health-marketed products actually score better than average. The real story: even so, roughly 1 in 3 still land a poor grade.

Filter by the claim on the pack, search a brand, or sort by any nutrient. Every row is a product positioned as healthy — the grade and sugar are what it actually contains.
